Living Oracles Global Institute Launches

Living Oracles Global Institute is pleased to announce its official launch, marking a new era in faith-based education. After years of prayer, planning, and preparation, LOGI opens its doors to students from around the world who are called to make a difference in their communities and beyond.

A Vision Realized

The vision for Living Oracles Global Institute was born out of a deep conviction that education must be both practical and Christ-centered. In a world where leaders are needed more than ever, LOGI exists to equip a generation of leaders who combine theological depth, business acumen, technological competence, and servant leadership.

Our programs are designed for working professionals, ministers, entrepreneurs, and aspiring leaders who want to develop their skills without putting their lives on hold. With flexible online learning, accessible tuition, and a globally recognized curriculum, LOGI is making quality faith-based education available to everyone.

"We are not just building an institution; we are raising a generation of leaders who will transform their communities, nations, and the world for the Kingdom of God." — Dr. E. Mensah, Founder, LOGI

What We Offer

Our programs combine theological depth with practical skills in business, technology, and leadership. Each programme is designed to develop knowledge, competence, and Christ-like character through:

  • Learn: Engaging video lectures and reading materials developed by experienced faculty
  • Practise: Real-world assignments and case studies that build practical competence
  • Build: Portfolio development that demonstrates your skills to employers and ministry partners
  • Solve: Problem-solving projects that address real challenges in your context
  • Portfolio: A professional portfolio showcasing your work and achievements
  • Certificate: An accredited certificate that opens doors to new opportunities

Our Academic Schools

LOGI is organized into four academic schools, each designed to serve a specific calling and career path:

  • School of Theology & Ministry: Equipping pastors, ministers, and church leaders with biblical depth and practical ministry skills
  • School of Business & Leadership: Developing business professionals and leaders who operate with integrity and excellence
  • School of Technology & Innovation: Training technologists and innovators who can build solutions that serve people
  • School of Foundational Studies: Building the critical thinking, communication, and foundational skills needed in every field

Global Reach, Local Impact

While LOGI is based in Ghana with headquarters in Kumasi, our students come from across Africa and around the world. Our online platform allows us to reach students wherever they are, while our local partnerships ensure that the education is contextual and relevant.

We believe that when leaders are equipped with faith, excellence, and impact, they can transform their communities—whether they are serving in a church, a business, a government, or a non-profit organization.
