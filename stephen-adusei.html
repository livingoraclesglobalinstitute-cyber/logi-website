Biography · Ministry · Author

Prophet Ing. Stephen Adusei

Christian Minister · Chemical Engineer · Educator · Author · Founder of LOGI

Some journeys are shaped by education. Others are shaped by experience, loss, faith, and a deeper search for purpose. My journey brings these experiences together.

Prophet Ing. Stephen Adusei is a Ghanaian Christian minister, teacher, chemical engineer, author, and founder of Living Oracles Global Institute (LOGI).

With an academic background spanning chemical engineering, biblical studies, theology, and business administration, he brings together professional knowledge, educational experience, and a commitment to Christian ministry.

Guiding message: "We cannot keep everything we build, but we can faithfully deposit something eternal into the lives of others."

Early Life, Faith & Personal Journey

Stephen's understanding of life and purpose has been shaped by his personal experiences, faith, and reflections on Scripture.

The loss of both parents became a defining experience in his personal reflections. Their passing led him to confront difficult questions about life, death, achievement, and eternity.

Through grief and reflection on Scripture, he began to appreciate that the value of a life is not measured merely by material possessions or personal accomplishments, but also by faith, character, love, and what a person contributes to the lives of others.

These reflections became the foundation of his book, When Everything We Build Must Eventually Be Left Behind, published in 2026.

Ministry & Biblical Teaching

As a Christian minister, Prophet Ing. Stephen Adusei is committed to communicating biblical truth and encouraging people to develop a deeper understanding of their faith.

His ministry interests include biblical teaching, spiritual growth, Christian leadership, personal development, and the practical application of Scripture to everyday life.

Through writing, teaching, and ministry-related activities, he seeks to encourage people to examine their lives in the light of God's Word and pursue a life characterized by faith, wisdom, integrity, and service.

His message emphasizes that Christian faith should influence not only what people believe but also how they live, lead, work, and relate to others.

Education & Professional Background

Stephen has an academic background in chemical engineering, biblical studies, and theology, together with further studies and professional development in business and leadership-related fields.

His professional experience includes industrial operations, teaching, academic support, and educational coordination.

These experiences have contributed to his interest in practical education and the development of individuals who can combine knowledge with competence, responsibility, and ethical values.

He believes education should do more than provide qualifications. It should equip people to solve problems, exercise sound judgment, develop their abilities, and contribute positively to their communities.

Founder of Living Oracles Global Institute

Prophet Ing. Stephen Adusei is the founder of Living Oracles Global Institute (LOGI), a Christ-centered online educational institution.

LOGI brings together theology, leadership, business, and technology through educational programmes designed to develop knowledge, practical competence, and responsible leadership.

His vision for the institution reflects his belief that biblical principles and professional development can work together to equip people for effective service in ministry, business, and society.

Through LOGI, he seeks to contribute to an educational environment where faith, practical learning, and leadership development complement one another.

Author & Inspirational Writer

Stephen is the author of When Everything We Build Must Eventually Be Left Behind, published in 2026 by Living Oracles Global Institute.

The book explores the temporary nature of human life and encourages readers to think about death, eternity, faith, personal responsibility, and the legacy they leave behind.

Drawing on personal reflection and biblical passages, the book invites readers to reconsider what truly matters and how they can use their time, resources, abilities, and relationships meaningfully.

Its central message is that although people cannot permanently retain everything they build or acquire, they can invest in others and leave a lasting legacy through faithful living and service.
