Curriculum Overview

Four core courses designed to build foundational biblical knowledge, interpretive skills, and theological understanding.

CBS 101 – Old Testament Survey 3 Credits

Explores the books, themes, and theological significance of the Old Testament, including the Pentateuch, historical books, poetry, and prophets, emphasizing God's redemptive plan.

Pentateuch · Historical Books · Wisdom Literature · Prophets

CBS 102 – New Testament Survey 3 Credits

Examines the Gospels, Acts, Epistles, and Revelation, focusing on the life of Christ, the early church, apostolic teaching, and the foundations of Christian faith.

Gospels · Acts · Pauline & General Epistles · Revelation

CBS 103 – Principles of Biblical Interpretation 3 Credits

Introduces sound hermeneutical principles, including historical-grammatical interpretation, literary context, genre analysis, and faithful application of Scripture.

Hermeneutics · Exegesis · Contextual Interpretation · Application

CBS 104 – Christian Doctrine & Theology 3 Credits

Covers core Christian doctrines including the Trinity, Christology, salvation, the Holy Spirit, the church, and eschatology, providing a theological foundation for ministry.

Core Doctrines · Trinitarian Theology · Soteriology · Eschatology
