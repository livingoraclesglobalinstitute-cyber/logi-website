Our Partners

Living Oracles Global Institute values strategic partnerships that advance quality education, Christian leadership development, community transformation, and Kingdom impact. We believe that collaboration strengthens our mission of equipping Christ-centered leaders to serve God and humanity with excellence.

As we continue to grow, we are building partnerships with churches, ministries, nonprofit organizations, educational institutions, and community development organizations that share our vision and values.

Our Faith Partners

The Living Oracles of God

Serves as one of our foundational ministry partners, providing spiritual guidance, leadership development, biblical teaching, and ministry support. This partnership includes guest lectures, pastoral mentorship for our students, and collaborative community outreach programs. Through this partnership, we are committed to raising disciples and leaders who demonstrate Christ-like character and biblical excellence.

ADUSEI Foundation

Partners with Living Oracles Global Institute in promoting education, leadership development, community empowerment, and humanitarian initiatives. This partnership includes joint scholarship programs for underprivileged students and collaborative community development projects. Together, we seek to create opportunities that positively impact individuals, families, and communities through education, mentorship, and compassionate service.

Future Partnerships

Living Oracles Global Institute welcomes collaboration with churches, universities, ministries, businesses, nonprofit organizations, and international partners who share our commitment to Christian education, leadership development, research, innovation, and community transformation.

These partnerships represent areas of mutual mission, prayer, resource-sharing, and referral relationships. Unless expressly stated in a formal articulation agreement, these partnerships do not imply academic accreditation, credit transfer, or endorsement of LOGI's certificates by partner institutions. Students are encouraged to confirm directly with any partner institution regarding recognition of LOGI programs for further study or employment.

Together, we are building a global network committed to equipping leaders, transforming lives, and advancing God's Kingdom through education and service.
