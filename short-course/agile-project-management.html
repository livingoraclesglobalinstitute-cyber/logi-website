Introduction to Agile Project Management

★★★★★ Beginner Level|4–5 Hours|Online, Self-Paced
LOGI
Living Oracles Global Institute
School of Engineering & Technology

This course introduces Agile values and ways of organising work in short, feedback-driven cycles. Learners explore iterative delivery, collaboration, prioritisation and common Scrum concepts.

What you will learnAgile principles, iterative delivery, backlogs, sprint planning, daily coordination, reviews and retrospectives.
Course durationApproximately 4–5 hours of self-paced learning.
AssessmentKnowledge checks, practical scenarios and a final assessment.

Course Modules

  • Agile Mindset and Principles
  • Iterative and Incremental Delivery
  • Roles and Responsibilities
  • Backlogs, Sprints and Reviews
  • Feedback and Continuous Improvement

Why Take This Course?

This course gives learners a practical foundation in introduction to agile project management and introduces concepts that can be applied in study and the workplace.

  • Build foundational knowledge of modern industrial systems.
  • Understand how sensors, controllers and machines communicate.
  • Explore practical examples from real industrial environments.
  • Learn at your own pace, online.
  • Complete an assessment and qualify for the LOGI certificate under the course requirements.

Who Is This Course For?

Beginners, students, project team members and professionals working in changing environments.

No previous programming or industrial automation experience is required.
