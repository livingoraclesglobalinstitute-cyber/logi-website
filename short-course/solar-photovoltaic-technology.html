Introduction to Solar Photovoltaic Technology

Learn the basic operation of solar photovoltaic systems, including modules, inverters, batteries, system components and safety considerations.

Course Description

Learn the basic operation of solar photovoltaic systems, including modules, inverters, batteries, system components and safety considerations. The course is designed as a concise foundation rather than advanced professional training. Learners will explore essential ideas through clear explanations, guided examples and an applied mini case study.

DurationApproximately 4–6 hours of self-paced learning.
Learning formatOnline learning with reading, selected videos and practical activities.
AssessmentKnowledge checks and a final course assessment, subject to course requirements.

Course Modules

  • Module 1: Introduction and key concepts
  • Module 2: Principles, terminology and core components
  • Module 3: Methods, tools and practical applications
  • Module 4: Workplace examples, risks and good practice
  • Module 5: Review, case study and final assessment
This is an introductory learning experience. It does not replace regulated technical instruction, workplace-specific procedures, professional licensing or specialist certification.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, learners should be able to:

  • Explain the key concepts and terminology used in introduction to solar photovoltaic technology.
  • Identify common tools, methods, risks or activities relevant to the subject.
  • Interpret a simple workplace or industry-based scenario.
  • Apply introductory knowledge to a basic practical task or case study.
  • Recognise when specialist guidance, standards or further training may be required.

Target Audience

This course is suitable for beginners, students, recent graduates, technicians, supervisors, professionals seeking a refresher and anyone interested in developing foundational knowledge in energy, oil & gas technologies.

No advanced prior knowledge is required. Learners should be willing to engage with short readings, examples and the course assessment.
