Basics of Construction Safety

★★★★★ Beginner Level|4–5 Hours|Online, Self-Paced
LOGI
Living Oracles Global Institute
School of Engineering & Technology

This course introduces common construction-site hazards and the basic practices used to prevent injury and ill health. It covers planning, site organisation, personal protective equipment and high-risk activities.

What you will learnSite inductions, PPE, work at height, excavations, electrical hazards, lifting operations, housekeeping and emergency arrangements.
Course durationApproximately 4–5 hours of self-paced learning.
AssessmentKnowledge checks, practical scenarios and a final assessment.

Course Modules

  • Construction Safety Fundamentals
  • Site Induction and PPE
  • Working at Height and Excavations
  • Electrical, Lifting and Equipment Hazards
  • Emergency Preparedness and Reporting

Why Take This Course?

This course gives learners a practical foundation in basics of construction safety and introduces concepts that can be applied in study and the workplace.

  • Build foundational knowledge of modern industrial systems.
  • Understand how sensors, controllers and machines communicate.
  • Explore practical examples from real industrial environments.
  • Learn at your own pace, online.
  • Complete an assessment and qualify for the LOGI certificate under the course requirements.

Who Is This Course For?

Construction students, site workers, supervisors, contractors and people entering construction environments.

No previous programming or industrial automation experience is required.

Certificate of Completion

Learners who complete the required course activities and achieve the stated pass mark in the final assessment may receive a LOGI Certificate of Completion.
