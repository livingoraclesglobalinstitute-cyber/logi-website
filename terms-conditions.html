LOGI retains the authority to make structural changes to courses to: (a) comply with updated laws or regulatory expectations, (b) integrate minor software or system improvements that do not impact content delivery, (c) align with international educational standards and nomenclature. 4. Academic Withdrawal Policy

To officially withdraw from an active program and terminate your student contract, you must submit a formal withdrawal notification via email to academic@logi.education. Your communication must include your legal name, assigned Student ID, active Program, phone number, and primary email address.

We reserve the right to terminate your enrollment immediately if you: (a) submit fraudulent, misleading, or plagiarized admission credentials, (b) fail to maintain minimum academic study standards, or (c) act in violation of our institutional code of conduct or disrupt the collaborative learning experience of other students.

By participating in our volunteer lecturing programs at Living Oracles Global Institute (LOGI), you covenant and agree to the operational and ethical frameworks detailed below:

1. Faculty Eligibility Requirements

  • You must be of legal age (or provide parental/guardian authorization) to participate in our international programs.
  • You must be a practicing Christian and hold doctrinal beliefs consistent with core biblical values and Christian teachings.
  • You must possess verified, relevant academic expertise, structural knowledge, or deep professional experience in the syllabus material you are assigned to teach.

You agree to dedicate your time, research, and effort to prepare, structure, and deliver high-quality instruction on topics assigned by our academic registry.
