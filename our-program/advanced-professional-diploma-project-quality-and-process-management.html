Areas of Study

Based on the programme description, learners will engage with the following subject areas:

  • Advanced project management, quality assurance, risk management, strategic planning,

Why Choose This Programme?

This programme provides structured learning in advanced project management, quality assurance, risk management, strategic planning, and capstone project. It is designed to connect foundational knowledge with practical situations in organisations, communities or ministry settings.

  • Study online through LOGI’s learning environment.
  • Develop knowledge related to the programme’s stated subject areas.
  • Connect learning to practical organisational, community or ministry contexts.
  • Review programme access and requirements through the LOGI Learning Portal.

Who Is This Programme For?

Students, professionals, project team members and individuals interested in developing practical business analysis, quality, project and process improvement skills.

The programme is suitable for learners seeking structured study in the subject area described above. Contact LOGI if you need guidance on entry requirements or programme suitability.
