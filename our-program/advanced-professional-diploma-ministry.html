Areas of Study

Based on the programme description, learners will engage with the following subject areas:

  • Advanced pastoral leadership, spiritual formation, discipleship strategies,
  • Capstone project

Why Choose This Programme?

This programme provides structured learning in advanced pastoral leadership, spiritual formation, discipleship strategies, and capstone project. It is designed to connect foundational knowledge with practical situations in organisations, communities or ministry settings.

  • Study online through LOGI’s learning environment.
  • Develop knowledge related to the programme’s stated subject areas.
  • Connect learning to practical organisational, community or ministry contexts.
  • Review programme access and requirements through the LOGI Learning Portal.

Who Is This Programme For?

Pastors, ministry workers, church leaders, volunteers, Bible students and individuals seeking to develop knowledge and practical skills for Christian service.

The programme is suitable for learners seeking structured study in the subject area described above. Contact LOGI if you need guidance on entry requirements or programme suitability.

Programme Award

This programme is listed by LOGI as an Advanced Professional Diploma. Please consult the LOGI admissions team or the Learning Portal for the applicable completion, assessment and award requirements.
