Curriculum Overview

Four core courses designed to build foundational Christian ministry and leadership competencies.

CCM 101 – Foundations of Christian Ministry 4 Credits

Introduction to the biblical, theological, and historical foundations of Christian ministry. Covers the nature and purpose of ministry, the call to serve, ministry ethics, and the role of the Holy Spirit in empowering effective service.

Ministry Foundations · Call to Serve · Ministry Ethics · Holy Spirit

CCM 102 – Biblical Interpretation & Teaching 4 Credits

Training in sound principles of biblical interpretation (hermeneutics) and effective teaching methods. Covers exegesis, contextualization, lesson preparation, and communicating biblical truth with clarity and relevance.

Hermeneutics · Exegesis · Contextualization · Teaching Methods

CCM 103 – Spiritual Formation & Discipleship 4 Credits

Focuses on personal spiritual growth and the practice of discipleship. Topics include prayer, spiritual disciplines, mentoring relationships, small group leadership, and developing others in their faith journey.

Spiritual Formation · Discipleship · Prayer · Mentoring

CCM 104 – Preaching & Ministry Communication 3 Credits

Develops skills in sermon preparation, preaching, and public communication in ministry contexts. Covers sermon structure, delivery techniques, engaging diverse audiences, and communicating with conviction and compassion.

Preaching · Sermon Preparation · Public Communication · Delivery Techniques
