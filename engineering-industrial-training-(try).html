Living Oracles Global Institute provides continuing education opportunities for learners seeking job-relevant technical knowledge across chemical and process engineering, manufacturing, plant operations and industrial technology.

Our initial training priorities draw on practical industry areas including solvent extraction, edible oil processing, water treatment, laboratory testing, quality control and recycling operations. Additional specialist programmes can be delivered with appropriately qualified instructors and industry partners.

Our practical learning focus

Understand industrial equipment, interpret technical information, investigate operating problems and develop the confidence to apply engineering principles in realistic work situations.

Our Learning Philosophy

Learn More Than Theory.
Develop Practical Competence.

Six connected steps that turn knowledge into real-world capability. We don't just teach you what to think; we teach you how to apply it effectively in your career and ministry.

1. Learn

Structured knowledge and principles delivered through accessible, high-quality online learning materials.

Technical exercises and guided application to reinforce what you've learned and build muscle memory.

Applied projects and case-based work that challenge you to construct real solutions.

Tangible evidence of your skills. Build a professional portfolio you can show to employers or congregations.

Learning with responsibility

Practical Skills with a Focus on Safety & Competence

Industrial work involves equipment, energy sources and operating hazards. LOGI's training approach places emphasis on safe working principles, technical understanding, quality awareness and responsible practice.

Where a course requires physical equipment operation or workshop competence, learners should complete those activities under appropriate supervision in a suitable training environment. Online study, simulations and recorded demonstrations do not replace hands-on workshop assessment or any required statutory trade qualification.

Flexible learning, clearly described

Each programme should state whether its practical component is delivered through simulation, recorded demonstration, supervised workshop activity or workplace experience.
