Institutional Integrity Statement

At Living Oracles Global Institute, we are deeply committed to fostering a culture of integrity rooted in biblical truth, Christian values, and academic excellence. Our unwavering commitment to honesty, transparency, accountability, and ethical conduct forms the foundation of our institution. This Institutional Integrity Statement reflects our dedication to representing ourselves truthfully and serving our students, partners, and the wider community with excellence and integrity.

Our Core Principles

Spiritual Foundation

We believe that every decision, policy, and action should reflect the teachings of Jesus Christ and the principles of God's Word. Our institution is guided by biblical values that promote faith, wisdom, servant leadership, and love.

Academic Integrity

We uphold the highest standards of academic honesty, ethical scholarship, and intellectual responsibility. Students, faculty, and staff are expected to demonstrate integrity in learning, teaching, research, assessment, and all academic activities.

Transparent Communication

We are committed to honest, accurate, and timely communication with students, staff, partners, and the public. We provide clear information about our programmes, admissions, tuition, policies, accreditation status, and developments while addressing inquiries promptly.

Ethical Practices

We conduct all institutional activities with fairness, professionalism, and accountability. Our admissions, financial operations, governance, employment, and student services are guided by biblical ethics, legal compliance, and respect for every individual.

Accountability

We accept responsibility for our actions and decisions. When mistakes occur, we are committed to acknowledging them, making appropriate corrections, and continually improving our systems, policies, and services.

Commitment to Students

Educational Excellence

We are dedicated to providing high-quality, Christ-centred education that equips students with biblical knowledge, professional skills, leadership capacity, and moral character for lifelong service and success.

Supportive Learning Environment

We strive to create a welcoming, inclusive, and spiritually enriching environment where every student is encouraged to grow academically, personally, professionally, and spiritually.

Honest Representation

We are committed to accurately representing our academic programmes, faculty, partnerships, learning resources, and institutional achievements. Students can rely on truthful and transparent information throughout their educational journey.

Commitment to the Public

Community Engagement

We actively engage with churches, ministries, organizations, and communities by promoting education, leadership development, discipleship, and social responsibility. We seek to be a positive influence through meaningful partnerships and faithful service.

Responsible Stewardship

We recognize that all resources entrusted to us are gifts from God. Therefore, we are committed to managing our financial, physical, technological, and human resources responsibly, transparently, and in ways that advance our educational mission.

Our Pledge

Living Oracles Global Institute remains steadfast in its commitment to integrity, accountability, biblical truth, and continuous improvement. We believe that faithful stewardship, ethical leadership, and unwavering honesty are essential to fulfilling our mission of equipping leaders, transforming lives, and advancing God's Kingdom through Christ-centred education.
