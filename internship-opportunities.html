Who Can Apply?

Applicants should be motivated to learn and ready to take part in supervised practical assignments.

  • Currently enrolled in a university, technical university, HND or relevant tertiary programme, or a recent graduate.
  • Interest in the selected internship area.
  • Good written and verbal communication skills.
  • Willingness to learn, accept guidance and complete assigned tasks.
  • Ability to work independently in a remote environment.
  • Basic computer and internet skills.
  • Google Workspace or Microsoft Office knowledge is an advantage.
  • Access to reliable internet and a suitable working environment.

Internship Structure

Practical Learning

Guided tasks, real organisational workflows and hands-on learning activities.

Supervision

Interns receive guidance and feedback from an assigned LOGI supervisor.

Progress Review

Work and learning progress are reviewed during and at the end of the placement.

Important: This is a student learning and professional-development placement, not a regular employment position. Any remuneration or allowance arrangements, if applicable, will be explained clearly before an applicant accepts a placement. Students who require formal industrial attachment documentation should indicate this when applying. Eligible interns may receive completion documentation after meeting the programme requirements.

How to Apply

To apply, email your application and include the following details:

  • Full name and contact details
  • Educational institution
  • Programme of study and academic level
  • Preferred internship position
  • CV or résumé
  • Industrial attachment letter, if applicable
