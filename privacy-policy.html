Your Privacy Rights

Data Protection Rights

For granular control over cookies, detailed management guidelines can be found at your web browser's respective official websites. We want to ensure you are fully aware of your data protection rights:

Right to Rectification

You have the right to request that we correct any personal data you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.

Right to Erasure

You may request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions and subject to legal or operational guidelines.

Right to Object

You reserve the right to object to our direct processing of your personal data under specific statutory conditions.

Data Portability

You have the right to request that we transfer collected data directly to another organization or to you.

Online Safety

Children's Information

Protecting younger users online is a foundational priority. We highly encourage parents, guardians, and mentors to observe, participate in, and actively guide their children's online educational activities.

Living Oracles Global Institute (LOGI) does not knowingly collect any Personally Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has inadvertently registered such information on our portal, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately, and we will apply our best efforts to promptly remove such records.

Compliance Framework

Family Educational Rights & Privacy Act (FERPA)

Living Oracles Global Institute complies with the principles of the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) alongside international education standards to fully safeguard student educational records.

01

Your Rights

  • Inspect and review your formal educational files within 45 days of a formal submission.
  • Request corrections to any details perceived as misleading, outdated, or inaccurate.
  • Provide express written authorization prior to any third-party disclosure, except as permitted by law.
  • File institutional complaints directly regarding compliance processes.
02

Permitted Disclosures

LOGI may selectively release education records without consent strictly to:

  • Authorized institutional officials demonstrating legitimate educational interests.
  • Official global accrediting and assessment bodies.
  • Legal entities as mandated by emergency situations or financial aid protocols.
03

Directory & Security

Directory Information: LOGI may safely publish general directory items (name, program of study, enrollment status, degrees awarded) unless a student explicitly submits a written opt-out notice to our Registrar.

Data Security: We enforce rigid technical, administrative, and physical safeguards across all digital platforms to prevent unauthorized access and uphold the absolute confidentiality of your records.
