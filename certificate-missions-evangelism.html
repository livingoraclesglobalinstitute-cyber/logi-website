Certificate in Missions & Evangelism (CME)

School of Ministry & Theology

The Certificate in Missions & Evangelism (CME) is a three-month programme designed for individuals called to cross-cultural ministry, missions, evangelism, and community outreach. The programme provides foundational knowledge in the theology of missions, cross-cultural communication, evangelism, church planting, justice, mercy ministry, and sustainable outreach.

Offered entirely online through our flexible e-learning model, this programme provides an accessible pathway for aspiring missionaries, evangelists, church planters, ministry leaders, and believers seeking to develop practical skills for effective outreach. Learners are equipped to communicate the Gospel across cultural contexts, engage communities with compassion, and contribute meaningfully to sustainable Christian mission initiatives.

Curriculum Overview

Four core courses designed to build foundational missions and evangelism competencies.

CME 101 – Theology of Missions & Evangelism 4 Credits

Explores the biblical and theological foundations of missions and evangelism, including the Great Commission, the missional nature of God, the role of the Holy Spirit, and the church's mandate to reach the nations with the Gospel.

Topics: Missiology, Great Commission, Missional theology, Holy Spirit, Global missions

CME 102 – Cross-Cultural Communication & Ministry 4 Credits

Equips learners with principles of cross-cultural communication, cultural intelligence, contextualization, and effectively sharing the Gospel across diverse cultural contexts without compromising biblical truth.

Topics: Cross-cultural communication, Cultural intelligence, Contextualization, Gospel communication

CME 103 – Evangelism Strategies & Discipleship 4 Credits

Covers practical approaches to personal and public evangelism, discipleship models, follow-up strategies, and equipping others to share their faith effectively in various settings.

Topics: Evangelism methods, Discipleship models, Follow-up strategies, Faith sharing

CME 104 – Church Planting, Justice & Sustainable Outreach 3 Credits

Introduces principles of church planting, community engagement, mercy ministry, social justice, and developing sustainable mission initiatives that address both spiritual and practical needs.

Topics: Church planting, Community engagement, Mercy ministry, Social justice, Sustainable outreach

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How long does the Certificate in Missions & Evangelism take?

❓ Are scholarships available?
