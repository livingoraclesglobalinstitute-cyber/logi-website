Equipped for Faithful Service

The School of Ministry & Theology exists to form men and women who know the Scriptures, love the Church, and serve with wisdom, humility, and competence.

Whether you are called to pastoral ministry, missions, church leadership, or community care, our programmes are designed to deepen your biblical understanding and sharpen your practical ministry skills.

Learn from experienced Christian educators and ministry practitioners who will walk with you through every step of your journey.
