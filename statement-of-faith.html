Our Biblical Foundation

Living Oracles Global Institute is committed to biblical truth, Christ-centred education, spiritual growth, and equipping believers for effective ministry and leadership.

The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Old and New Testament Scriptures are the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God, revealing His will and serving as the final authority for faith and Christian living.

Jesus Christ

We believe in the absolute Lordship and Divinity of Jesus Christ, His eternal existence, virgin birth, sinless life, substitutionary death, bodily resurrection, ascension, and glorious second coming.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the ministry of the Holy Spirit, who regenerates, sanctifies, transforms, guides, and empowers believers for holy living and effective ministry.

The Triune God

We believe there is one true and living God who eternally exists as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—three distinct Persons in perfect unity.

Salvation by Grace

We believe salvation is the free gift of God through grace by faith in Jesus Christ. His finished work on the Cross paid the full price for humanity's redemption.

The New Creation

We believe every believer is a new creation in Christ and is empowered to live a victorious Christian life through His finished work.

The Church

We believe the Church is the Body of Christ, united in faith without division, called to worship God, serve one another, and proclaim the Gospel.

World Evangelism

We believe the Great Commission calls every believer to make disciples of all nations until Jesus Christ returns.

Holy Living

We believe holiness, godly character, Christian conduct, faithful stewardship, and sincere worship are essential responsibilities of every believer.

Eternal Destiny

We believe every person will exist forever. Those who receive Jesus Christ will enjoy eternal life with God, while those who reject Him will experience eternal separation from His presence.
