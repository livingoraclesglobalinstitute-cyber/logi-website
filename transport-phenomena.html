Module 1: Fundamentals

Transport phenomena is a branch of chemical engineering that studies how three fundamental quantities move from one location to another: momentum, energy (heat), and mass.

1. Momentum Transfer

Momentum transfer deals with the movement of momentum within fluids. When a fluid flows through a pipe, different layers move at different velocities, producing internal friction known as viscosity.
Newton's Law of Viscosity: τ = μ (du/dy)

2. Heat Transfer

Heat transfer is the movement of thermal energy from a region of higher temperature to a lower temperature.
Fourier's Law: q = -kA (dT/dx)

3. Mass Transfer

Mass transfer involves the movement of chemical species due to concentration differences.
Fick's First Law: JA = -DAB (dCA/dx)
