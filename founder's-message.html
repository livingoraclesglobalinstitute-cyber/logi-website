Founder's Message

Raising Christ-Centered Leaders for Global Impact

"A perfect blend of Leadership, Business, Technology & Theology"

The future of our communities, institutions, churches, businesses, and nations depends greatly on the quality of leaders we develop. We need leaders who are not only educated, but equipped with biblical wisdom, practical competence, ethical values, and the character to lead with integrity.

— Prophet Ing. Stephen Adusei, Founder

The future of our communities, institutions, churches, businesses, and nations depends greatly on the quality of leaders we develop. We need leaders who are not only educated, but equipped with biblical wisdom, practical competence, ethical values, and the character to lead with integrity. In a rapidly changing world, there is a growing need for leaders who can combine faith with knowledge, professional excellence with servant leadership, and conviction with practical action.

When we recognised the growing gap between faith and practice, knowledge and competence, and education and transformational leadership, we were inspired to establish Living Oracles Global Institute (LOGI) — a Christ-centered online institution committed to equipping individuals for transformational leadership through high-quality biblical and professional education. We exist to make practical, Christ-centered education accessible to people who desire to grow in ministry, leadership, business, technology, and professional service.

At LOGI, we believe true education extends far beyond examinations or the acquisition of knowledge. Education should develop the whole person — strengthening understanding, critical thinking, practical application, spiritual maturity, ethical leadership, and Christ-like character. Our approach brings together biblical wisdom, academic excellence, practical competence, and real-world application to prepare leaders who can serve effectively and make a lasting difference.

Our programmes bring together Theology, Ministry, Leadership, Business, Technology, and professional disciplines in a flexible online learning environment designed for today's learners. Through practical, outcomes-based education, we equip students to apply what they learn, solve real-world challenges, lead with wisdom and integrity, and use their God-given potential to serve their churches, communities, professions, and nations.

We are particularly passionate about developing leaders who will carry Christian values beyond the walls of the church — into business, education, technology, public service, organisations, communities, and every sphere of society. We believe that when biblical truth is combined with competence, character, and excellence, education can become a powerful instrument for transformation.

We are raising Christ-centered leaders one person at a time — leaders who are grounded in biblical truth, equipped with practical knowledge, committed to ethical leadership, and prepared to make a meaningful global impact.

Your calling deserves preparation. Your faith should influence how you lead. Your knowledge should produce impact.

WE ARE RAISING CHRIST-CENTERED LEADERS FOR GLOBAL IMPACT.
