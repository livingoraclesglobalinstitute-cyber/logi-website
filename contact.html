FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions we receive.

How do I apply for admission to LOGI?

You can apply directly through our online application form at enrol.logi.education. The process is fully online, and you'll need to provide your personal details, educational background, and select your program of interest.

What programs does LOGI offer?

We offer certificates and diplomas through three schools: the School of Business & Leadership, the School of Ministry & Theology, and the School of Engineering & Technology. Visit our Our Programs page for the full list.

Are scholarships available?

Yes! LOGI is committed to making Christian education affordable. We offer various scholarship opportunities and financial aid options. Please contact us at finance@logi.education for more details.

How long does it take to complete a program?

Program duration varies. Most certificate programs can be completed in 2-3 months, while advanced diplomas typically take 5-6 months. Since our programs are self-paced online, you can progress at a speed that works for you.

Can I study from outside Ghana?

Absolutely! LOGI is a fully online institution serving students worldwide. As long as you have internet access, you can study from anywhere in the world.

How can I partner with LOGI?

We welcome partnerships with churches, ministries, organizations, and individuals who share our vision. Please reach out to us at partnerships@logi.education to explore collaboration opportunities.
