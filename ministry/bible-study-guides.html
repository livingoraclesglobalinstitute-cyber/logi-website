How Our Study Guides Are Structured

Every lesson follows the same simple structure — so whether you're studying alone or leading a group, you know exactly what to expect and how to engage with the passage.

Evaluate

Observe the text — who, what, where, when, and the problem being addressed.

Learn

Discover the main theme, the sin to confess, and the love of God in the passage.

Lead

Apply the truth — how will you live it out and share it with others?

Discussion

Go deeper — wrestle with hard questions and related Scripture passages.

Wrap-Up

Restate the lesson goal and reflect on what God has taught you.
