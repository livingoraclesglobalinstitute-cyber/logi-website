Why We Give

Giving is an act of worship. It is the joyful response of a grateful heart — an opportunity to partner with God in His work of reaching the lost, strengthening the church, and advancing His kingdom on earth.

When you give to LOGI Ministry, you are not just supporting an organisation. You are sowing into eternal work. Every gift — large or small — helps take the gospel to the ends of the earth.

2 Corinthians 9:7 (KJV)

"Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver."

An Act of Worship

Giving is not about money — it is about the heart. When we give generously, we declare that God is first in our lives and that we trust Him to provide.

A Kingdom Investment

Every gift you give is invested in eternal work — souls saved, believers discipled, churches strengthened, and the gospel preached to the ends of the earth.

An Act of Obedience

Scripture calls us to give faithfully and generously. Giving is not optional for the believer — it is part of following Jesus with our whole lives.

A Blessing to You

God promises to bless those who give. Not always with material wealth — but with joy, peace, and the deep satisfaction of knowing you are part of something eternal.

Where Your Gift Goes

Every gift given to LOGI Ministry is used prayerfully and wisely to advance the gospel. Here are the four main areas your generosity supports:

Evangelism

Supporting gospel outreach, evangelism training, and digital campaigns that bring the good news of Jesus to those who have never heard.

Missions

Supporting missionaries, church planters, and outreach work in hard-to-reach places — reaching unreached people groups with the love of Jesus.

Bible Teaching

Providing free Bible studies, devotionals, sermons, and discipleship resources to believers around the world — at no cost to them.

Discipleship

Helping new believers grow and mature believers go deeper — through teaching on prayer, Christian living, marriage, and spiritual growth.
