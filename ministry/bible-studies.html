Bible Studies

Explore Scripture · Grow in Truth

Where do you begin?

Discover the message of the Bible, ask honest questions, and learn how God's Word shapes faith, character, prayer, relationships, and everyday decisions. These guided studies are designed for personal devotion, families, small groups, and church fellowships.

Start with a topic that speaks to your season of life. Open a study to find its key passages, guiding questions, and a practical response. Read the full passages in your Bible and allow Scripture to shape your conclusions.

2 Timothy 2:15 (KJV) — "Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth."

The Good News of Jesus Christ

Understand the human need for salvation and the hope offered through Jesus.

Romans 3:23–24; 5:1–8

Trace the Bible's message of sin, grace, faith, reconciliation, and new life in Christ.

Read: Romans 5:1–11 in your Bible. Related: Romans 3:21–26; Ephesians 2:1–10.

  7. Is the problem solved?

  4. What does God teach us to do?

  1. What would be a good situation to share this message?

Discussion

  1. According to Romans 5, what does it mean to be justified by faith?

Goal: Can I explain how God offers forgiveness and new life through Jesus Christ?

These guides are starting points for reading and discussion, not substitutes for reading the Bible passage in context. Compare related verses, consider the original passage's setting, and seek wise pastoral guidance when a question needs further study.

How to Study the Bible

You do not need to be a Bible scholar to begin studying Scripture. Start with prayer, read the passage more than once, and pay attention to what the text actually says.

STEP 01

Pray

Ask God for humility, understanding, and a willingness to obey what you learn. See Psalm 119:18.

STEP 02

Observe

Who is speaking? What is happening? Notice repeated words, commands, promises, and contrasts.

STEP 03

Understand

Read the surrounding verses and consider the book's setting. Compare Scripture with Scripture.

STEP 04

Apply

Identify one truthful, practical response. Share what you learned and return to the passage during the week.
