Bible Stories by Category

Stories That Teach · Truths That Transform

Why study Bible stories?

Many passages in Scripture are teaching passages — letters, sermons, and instructions. But the Bible is also full of stories: real people in real situations, facing real choices. When we pair a teaching passage with a Bible story, the truth becomes concrete and memorable.

Each story below opens into a full guided study using the THINK → EVALUATE → LEARN → LEAD → DISCUSSION → Goal framework. Click a story to open it. Opening a new story closes the previous one.
