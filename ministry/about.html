About

Who We Are

LOGI Ministry is the ministry arm of Living Oracles Global Institute — a Christ-centred ministry devoted to knowing Christ, following Christ, and making Christ known. We exist to help believers grow in their walk with Jesus through Bible teaching, discipleship, prayer, evangelism, and missions.

Our heart is simple: to see lives transformed by the gospel, to see believers rooted in God's Word, to see the church equipped for works of ministry, and to see the name of Jesus lifted up in every nation.

Our Mission

To help every believer know Christ personally, grow in Him deeply, and make Him known boldly — through Bible teaching, discipleship, prayer, evangelism, and missions.

Our Vision

A world where every person has the opportunity to hear the gospel, every believer is growing as a disciple of Jesus, and every church is equipped for the work of the ministry.

Our Values

We are committed to the authority of Scripture, the centrality of the cross, the work of the Holy Spirit, the priority of prayer, and the urgency of the Great Commission.

Our Story

LOGI Ministry was born out of Living Oracles Global Institute — a Christ-centred institution that had already been training leaders for ministry and the marketplace. This ministry was added to reach beyond the classroom and serve the wider body of Christ.

What We Do

Our ministry is focused on six key areas — all designed to help you know Christ, follow Christ, and make Christ known.

Bible Teaching

We publish Bible studies, sermons, devotionals, and teaching resources to help believers understand and apply God's Word.

Discipleship

We help new believers grow and mature believers go deeper — through resources on prayer, Christian living, marriage, and spiritual growth.

Prayer

We stand in prayer for the nations, the church, and the needs of God's people — and we invite you to share your prayer requests with us.

Evangelism

We equip believers to share the gospel with confidence — at home, at work, in their communities, and online.

Missions

We support missionaries, church planters, and outreach work around the world — reaching the unreached with the love of Jesus.

Ministries

We serve specific groups — youth, children, men, women, families — with resources and teaching tailored to their needs.

Our Commitment

We are committed to the authority of God's Word, the centrality of Jesus Christ, and the work of the Holy Spirit. Every sermon we preach, every article we publish, and every ministry we undertake is measured against the Scriptures.

We are not here to build our own name. We are here to lift up the name of Jesus — in every home, every community, and every nation — until He returns.
