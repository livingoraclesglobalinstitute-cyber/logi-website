New Believers

Starting Your Walk With Jesus

Welcome to the family of God!

If you have just received Jesus Christ as your Lord and Savior, then something miraculous has happened. Your sins are forgiven. You have been made new. You have been adopted into God's family. The Holy Spirit now lives inside you. And heaven itself is rejoicing over you.

But this is just the beginning. Like a newborn baby, you now need to grow — to be nourished, strengthened, and cared for. This page is your starting point. It will help you understand what has happened to you and how to take your first steps as a follower of Jesus.

What Just Happened to Me?

When you placed your faith in Jesus, several incredible things happened at once — whether you felt them or not. These are not things you need to earn or maintain. They are gifts given to you the moment you believed.

You Were Forgiven

Every sin — past, present, and future — has been completely forgiven through the blood of Jesus. There is no sin too great for His grace to cover.

You Were Adopted

You are now a child of God — not just a follower, but a son or daughter. You can call Him "Father," and you have a permanent place in His family.

You Were Given the Spirit

The Holy Spirit now lives inside you — teaching you, guiding you, convicting you, comforting you, and empowering you to live for God.

You Were Given Eternal Life

You have received a life that will never end — a relationship with God that begins now and continues forever, beyond even death itself.

2 Corinthians 5:17 (KJV)

"Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."

Romans 8:16 (KJV)

"The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God."

You are not the same person you were before. You are a new creation in Christ — and everything about your life is now being made new by His grace.

Your First Steps

Now that you belong to Christ, here are the four most important things to do as a new believer. These are not requirements to earn salvation — they are simple steps of obedience and love that will help you grow.

  1. Be baptized.

    Baptism is your first public act of obedience. It is how you tell the world — and yourself — that you belong to Jesus. Going under the water symbolizes being buried with Christ; coming up symbolizes being raised with Him to new life. If you haven't yet been baptized, this is your next step. See our Baptism teaching to understand what the Bible says.

  2. Read God's Word every day.

    The Bible is your spiritual food. Just as your body needs food each day, your spirit needs Scripture. Start with the Gospel of John, then Acts, then Romans — reading a little every day. Ask God to speak to you through what you read. See our Bible Studies for help getting started.

  3. Pray continually.

    Prayer is how you talk with God — honestly and often. You don't need fancy words. Tell Him what you're feeling, ask for what you need, thank Him for what He has done, and listen for His voice. Prayer is your lifeline. Learn more on our Prayer page.

  4. Join a Bible-believing church.

    God never intended for you to walk this journey alone. Find a Christ-centered, Bible-teaching church where you can worship, learn, serve, and grow alongside other believers. You need their encouragement — and they need yours. "Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together… but exhorting one another" (Hebrews 10:25).

What About When I Sin?

Here is an important truth every new believer needs to know: you will still sin. Becoming a Christian does not make you perfect overnight. Your old nature is still present, and you will still stumble at times. But here is the good news — your salvation does not depend on your performance. It depends on Christ's finished work on the cross.

When you sin, don't run from God. Run to Him. Confess your sin, receive His forgiveness, and keep walking forward. He is not waiting to condemn you — He is waiting to forgive you.

1 John 1:9 (KJV)

"If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness."

Romans 8:1 (KJV)

"There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit."

You do not need to fear that your failures will cost you your salvation. You are held securely in Christ's hand — and He will not let you go.

When You Struggle

Every believer struggles. Even the greatest heroes of the faith doubted, sinned, and stumbled. But your struggles do not define you — Christ does. When you stumble, come back to Him. When you doubt, remember His promises. When you feel distant, draw near to Him.

When You Doubt

Doubt is normal. Even John the Baptist once doubted (Matthew 11:2–3). Trust God's Word more than your feelings. Your salvation rests on His promises, not on your emotions.

When You Feel Distant From God

Feelings are not facts. God has promised never to leave you (Hebrews 13:5). If you feel distant, keep praying, keep reading His Word, and trust that He is near even when you don't feel Him.

When You Stumble Into Sin

Confess, receive His forgiveness, and keep walking. Do not let shame keep you from God. He already knows — and He already loves you. His grace is greater than your sin.

When You Feel Alone

You are not alone. You have the Holy Spirit living in you, and God designed you to be part of a church family. Reach out to other believers — you were never meant to walk this road alone.

When You Don't Know What to Do

Open God's Word. The Bible is your guide for every decision, every season, and every question. "Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path" (Psalm 119:105).

A Prayer for Your New Walk

Pray this prayer as you begin your journey with Jesus. Come back to it as often as you need to:

Pray This Prayer:

"Father, thank You for saving me. Thank You for forgiving my sins, for adopting me as Your child, and for giving me the Holy Spirit. I don't fully understand everything You have done — but I trust You.

Teach me to walk with You. Give me a hunger for Your Word and a desire to pray. Help me find a Bible-believing church where I can grow with other believers. Lead me to be baptized as an act of obedience.

When I stumble, help me to run to You, not away from You. When I doubt, strengthen my faith. When I feel alone, remind me that You are always with me.

I choose today to follow Jesus — not just once, but every day of my life. Make me more like Him. Amen."
