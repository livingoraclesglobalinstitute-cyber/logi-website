Evangelism

Sharing the Gospel With Confidence

Why We Share the Gospel

Evangelism is not a program — it is the joyful overflow of a heart that has been transformed by the love of Christ. When we truly understand what Jesus has done for us, we cannot help but tell others.

Every believer is called to be a witness. Not everyone is called to be an evangelist, but every follower of Jesus is called to share the good news of salvation — in their home, at their work, in their community, and wherever God places them.

Mark 16:15 (KJV)

"And he said unto them, Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature."

Because God Loves People

Every person on earth is made in the image of God — and He desires that none should perish. Evangelism is the overflow of God's love for the lost.

Because Jesus Commanded It

Before He ascended, Jesus gave His followers the Great Commission — to make disciples of all nations. Sharing the gospel is not optional; it is a command.

Because People Are Lost Without Christ

Without the gospel, people remain separated from God — eternally. This is the most urgent reality in all of life. We share because we care.

Because It Brings Joy

There is no greater joy than leading someone to Christ. Heaven itself rejoices when one sinner repents. Evangelism is not a burden — it is a privilege.

The Gospel in Four Steps

The gospel can be shared clearly and simply in four key truths. When you share your faith, keep these four steps in mind — they are the heart of the good news.

  1. God Is Holy and Loving.

    God created us for relationship with Him. He is perfectly holy, perfectly just, and perfectly loving. He desires that every person know Him and walk with Him.

  2. We Have All Sinned.

    Every one of us has rebelled against God's good design. Sin separates us from Him, and the wages of sin is death — eternal separation from God. None of us can save ourselves.

  3. Jesus Died and Rose Again.

    Out of love, God sent His Son. Jesus lived the perfect life we could not live, died the death we deserved on the cross, and rose again — defeating sin and death forever.

  4. He Offers You Life.

    God invites you to turn from sin and trust in Jesus. Through faith, you receive forgiveness, eternal life, and a new relationship with God. This is the free gift of the gospel.

Romans 3:23 (KJV)

"For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God."

Romans 5:8 (KJV)

"But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us."

Romans 10:9 (KJV)

"That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved."

How to Share Your Faith

Sharing the gospel does not require a seminary degree or a perfect script. It requires a willing heart, a clear message, and a dependence on the Holy Spirit. Here are some practical steps:

Pray First

Before you share with anyone, pray for them by name. Ask God to prepare their heart, to give you the right words, and to open a door for the conversation.

Start a Conversation

Evangelism usually begins with ordinary conversation. Ask questions, listen well, and look for opportunities to share what Christ has done in your own life.

Share Your Story

You don't need to know all the answers. Simply tell your story — what your life was like before Christ, how you met Him, and how He has changed you.

Share the Scriptures

Use key verses to explain the gospel — Romans 3:23, Romans 6:23, Romans 5:8, Romans 10:9. Let God's Word do the work; it never returns void.

Invite a Response

Don't leave the conversation hanging. Gently invite them to respond — to repent, believe, and receive Christ. Offer to pray with them right there.

Follow Up

If they receive Christ, help them take the next step — baptism, Bible reading, prayer, and joining a Bible-believing church. Discipleship follows evangelism.
