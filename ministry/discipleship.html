Discipleship

Growing in Your Walk With Christ

Following Jesus is not a one-time decision — it is a lifelong journey.

Salvation is the beginning of the Christian life, not the end. Once you have trusted in Jesus Christ, you are called to follow Him — to become His disciple. That word "disciple" means a learner, a student, an apprentice. A disciple is someone who is daily learning from Jesus, growing in Christlike character, and being transformed by the Holy Spirit.

This page will help you understand what discipleship is, why it matters, and how to grow deeper in your walk with Christ.

What Is Discipleship?

Discipleship is the lifelong process of becoming more like Jesus. It is not about being perfect — it is about being faithful. It is the daily choice to follow Christ, to learn from Him, to obey Him, and to let His Spirit reshape your heart and life.

When Jesus called His first disciples, He said two simple words: "Follow me." That invitation is still extended to every believer today. To be a disciple is to accept that call — not just once, but every day.

Luke 9:23

"Then he said to them all: 'Whoever wants to be my disciple must deny themselves and take up their cross daily and follow me.'"

John 15:8

"This is to my Father's glory, that you bear much fruit, showing yourselves to be my disciples."

Discipleship is not an event. It is a relationship — a living, growing walk with the living Christ. And like any relationship, it deepens over time through time spent together.

What Does a Disciple Look Like?

Discipleship has visible marks. Here are some of the qualities that appear in the life of a growing disciple of Jesus:

  1. They love God above all.

    Jesus said the greatest commandment is to "love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind" (Matthew 22:37). A disciple's life is ordered around this first love.

  2. They love people.

    Jesus said the second command is like the first: "Love your neighbor as yourself" (Matthew 22:39). A disciple loves people — even difficult people — because Christ first loved them.

  3. They obey God's Word.

    Jesus said, "If you love me, keep my commands" (John 14:15). A disciple doesn't just hear the Word — they put it into practice.

  4. They bear spiritual fruit.

    Jesus said, "By their fruit you will recognize them" (Matthew 7:16). The fruit of the Spirit — love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control — becomes visible in a disciple's life.

  5. They make other disciples.

    Discipleship is not meant to stop with you. Jesus said, "Go and make disciples of all nations" (Matthew 28:19). A mature disciple helps others grow in Christ too.

John 13:35 says, "By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you love one another."

How Do I Grow as a Disciple?

Spiritual growth is not automatic — but it happens naturally when you practice certain things consistently. Here are four practices every disciple should build into their life:

Spend Time With God Daily

Set aside time each day to read Scripture and pray. You don't need hours — a few focused minutes with God each morning will transform your walk over time.

Be Part of a Local Church

Discipleship does not happen in isolation. Find a Christ-centered, Bible-teaching church and commit to being part of its community. You will grow faster and further with others than alone.

Find a Mentor or Discipleship Group

Look for someone more mature in the faith who can walk alongside you. Or join a small group where you can learn from and encourage other believers. We all grow best in community.

Serve Others in Jesus' Name

Faith grows when it is put into action. Find a place to serve — in your church, your community, or online — and use your gifts to bless others.

Share Your Faith

A disciple who never shares their faith misses one of the greatest joys of following Jesus. Tell others what He has done in your life. Learn more on our Evangelism page.

A Prayer for Your Discipleship Journey

Pray this prayer as you commit to growing as a disciple of Jesus:

Pray This Prayer:

"Father, thank You for calling me to follow Jesus. I want to be a faithful disciple — not just a believer, but a learner, a follower, an apprentice of Your Son.

Teach me to love You with all my heart, to love others as myself, and to obey Your Word with joy.

Grow in me the fruit of Your Spirit, and help me bear much fruit for Your glory.

Lead me into deeper fellowship with You and with other believers. And give me opportunities to share the good news of Jesus with others.

I choose today to follow You — not just once, but every day. Lead me, guide me, and make me more like Christ.

Amen."
