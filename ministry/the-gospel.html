The Gospel

Good News for Every Soul

The word "gospel" means "good news."

But to understand why it's good news, we first need to understand the story. The gospel is not a set of rules to follow or a philosophy to admire. It is the true story of what God has done — through Jesus Christ — to rescue us, forgive us, and bring us back into a living relationship with Him.

This page walks you through the heart of that message. Read slowly. Read prayerfully. And when you're done, you'll find a clear invitation to respond.

What Is the Gospel?

The gospel is the message that God — who is holy, loving, and just — has acted in history to save sinners through the life, death, and resurrection of His Son, Jesus Christ. It is not something we earn, achieve, or deserve. It is a free gift offered to everyone who will receive it by faith.

The Bible summarizes it this way:

1 Corinthians 15:3–4 says, "For what I received I passed on to you as of first importance: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures."

This message unfolds in four great truths. Together, they tell the whole story of the gospel.

1. God Is Holy & Loving

God is perfectly holy, perfectly good, and perfectly loving. He created us for relationship with Him — to know Him, love Him, and walk with Him.

2. We Have Sinned

Every one of us has rebelled against God's good design. Sin separates us from Him, and the wages of sin is death — eternal separation from God.

3. Jesus Died & Rose Again

Out of love, God sent His Son. Jesus lived the perfect life we couldn't live, died the death we deserved, and rose again — defeating sin and death forever.

4. He Offers You Life

God invites you to turn from sin and trust in Jesus. Through faith, you receive forgiveness, eternal life, and a new relationship with God.

1. God Is Holy and Loving

Everything begins with God. He is not a distant force or an impersonal energy. He is a personal, loving Creator who made the universe, made us, and wants to be known by us.

He is also perfectly holy. That means He is completely pure, completely good, and completely just. There is no darkness, no evil, and no wrong in Him. His holiness is why sin matters so much — because sin is against Him.

Psalm 145:17

"The Lord is righteous in all his ways and faithful in all he does."

1 John 4:8

"Whoever does not love does not know God, because God is love."

2. We Have All Sinned

God created us for relationship with Him. But we chose — and continue to choose — our own way instead of His. The Bible calls this sin. Sin is not just doing bad things; it is the deeper rebellion of the human heart against God's rightful rule.

Because God is holy and just, sin must be dealt with. It cannot simply be ignored. And because every one of us has sinned, none of us can stand before God on our own merit.

Romans 3:23

"For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God."

Romans 6:23

"For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord."

This is the bad news that makes the good news so good. We are not basically good people who occasionally slip up. We are sinners who need a Savior. And God, in His mercy, provided one.

3. Jesus Died and Rose Again

Here is the heart of the gospel. Because He loves us, God did not leave us in our sin. He sent His Son, Jesus Christ, to do what we could never do.

Jesus lived the perfect, sinless life we could not live. He taught, healed, forgave, and loved. Then He willingly went to the cross, where He took the penalty for our sins — the death we deserved — in our place.

Three days later, Jesus rose from the grave. He conquered sin, death, and hell. His resurrection is God's declaration that the payment was accepted, the victory is won, and eternal life is available to all who believe.

Romans 5:8

"But God demonstrates his own love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us."

John 14:6

"Jesus answered, 'I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.'"

4. He Offers You Life

Because of what Jesus has done, God now extends a free invitation to every person: turn from your sin, trust in My Son, and receive eternal life.

This is not something you can earn by being good, religious, or morally upright. It is a gift — received by grace, through faith. When you place your trust in Jesus, everything changes. Your sins are forgiven. You are adopted into God's family. You receive the Holy Spirit. And you begin a new life that will never end.

John 3:16

"For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life."

Ephesians 2:8–9

"For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith — and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of God — not by works, so that no one can boast."

How Will You Respond?

The gospel is not merely information to consider. It is an invitation to respond. God is calling you — right now, wherever you are — to turn to Him and trust in Jesus.
