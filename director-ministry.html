Prophet Ing. Stephen Adusei

Prophet Ing. Stephen Adusei serves as the Director of the School of Ministry & Theology at Living Oracles Global Institute (LOGI). He is a versatile professional whose unique blend of engineering, theology, and administrative experience shapes his approach to leadership.

His work focuses on preparing students and professionals for impactful service in ministry, leadership, and the marketplace. He is passionate about developing leaders who can unite faith and knowledge, professional excellence with servant leadership, and conviction with practical action. His multidisciplinary background in chemical engineering, business, and theology informs his commitment to making practical, Christ-centered education accessible to those called to lead in every sphere of society.

"We are raising Christ-centered leaders for global impact — leaders grounded in biblical truth, equipped with practical knowledge, committed to ethical leadership, and ready to make a meaningful difference everywhere they go." — Prophet Ing. Stephen Adusei

Academic & Professional Background

Prophet Ing. Stephen Adusei holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) with specialisation in Leadership, Technology, and Theology and a Bachelor of Biblical Studies. He also holds a B Tech in Chemical Engineering and an HND in Chemical Engineering.

His professional credentials include certificates in Mobile and Online Church Ministry, Business Administration with specialisation in Leadership, Technology, and Theology, and a Diploma in Theology. He has also completed numerous short courses in project management, AI, leadership, quality management, and process optimisation from institutions including Google and Great Learning Academy.

His areas of professional interest include curriculum development, academic support, ministry leadership, and the integration of technology into theological education.

Leadership at LOGI

As Director of the School of Ministry & Theology, Prophet Ing. Stephen Adusei contributes to the academic and strategic development of LOGI's ministry-focused programmes. His responsibilities include:

  • Academic and curriculum development for ministry programmes
  • Theology programme coordination and oversight
  • Faculty collaboration and development
  • Student mentorship and spiritual formation
  • Development of practical and skills-based ministry education
  • Integration of technology into theological training

Through his leadership, the School of Ministry & Theology seeks to provide students with an educational experience that brings together biblical wisdom, academic excellence, practical competence, and real-world application to prepare leaders who can serve effectively and make a lasting difference.

Professional Vision

Prophet Ing. Stephen Adusei is passionate about developing ministry and theology education that goes beyond theoretical knowledge. His vision is to contribute to the development of competent, ethical, and spiritually mature professionals who can apply their knowledge to real-world challenges across Africa and the global community.

At LOGI, he supports the Institute's commitment to providing accessible, practical, and professionally relevant education that develops the whole person — strengthening understanding, critical thinking, practical application, spiritual maturity, ethical leadership, and Christ-like character.

His strategic vision for the School of Ministry & Theology is built around Learn → Practise → Build → Solve → Portfolio → Certificate, ensuring that students graduate with demonstrable skills, not just theoretical knowledge. He believes that when biblical truth is combined with competence, character, and excellence, education can become a powerful instrument for transformation.
