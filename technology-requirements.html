Technology Requirements

To ensure a seamless and productive learning experience, please verify that your system meets the minimum technical specifications listed below. Access to a reliable device, a stable internet connection, and the necessary software applications will allow you to participate effectively in live classes, complete assignments, and complete proctored examinations.

💻

Device

To participate in our courses effectively, you will need access to a computer or tablet with an internet connection.

🌐

Internet Connection

For optimal experience, we recommend a broadband connection (minimum 2mbps), especially for classes featuring video content.

📷

Web Camera (Webcam)

A webcam with a microphone is mandatory for proctored exams, student verification, and project defense purposes.

🧭

Browser

Any commonly used modern web browser will suffice, including Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and others.

📝

Word Processing

Microsoft Word or free structural alternatives like Google Docs, WPS Office, and LibreOffice are suitable options.

📄

Portable Document Format

Several course documents are provided in PDF format. You can download a free reader directly from Adobe Reader.

🚀

Mobile Applications (Zoom, Moodle etc.)

We conduct live classes via Zoom and utilize Moodle as our LMS. For your convenience, you can easily install these apps from the Google Play Store or Apple Store.
