Advanced Professional Diploma in Ministry (APDM)

School of Ministry & Theology

The Advanced Professional Diploma in Ministry (APDM) is a comprehensive 6–9 month advanced programme designed for pastors, ministry leaders, church planters, and missionaries seeking to deepen their leadership capacity and theological understanding. This advanced program covers pastoral leadership, spiritual formation, discipleship strategies, and culminates in a comprehensive capstone project that integrates all learning outcomes.

Offered entirely online through our flexible e-learning model, this programme provides an accessible pathway for ministry leaders seeking to elevate their ministry impact and leadership effectiveness in today's dynamic ministry environment.

Curriculum Overview

Eight core courses designed to build advanced ministry leadership competencies.

MTH 501 – Advanced Pastoral Leadership 4 Credits

Advanced exploration of pastoral leadership principles, church governance, and ministry administration. Covers leading congregations, managing ministry teams, navigating complex pastoral challenges, and developing leadership capacity in others.

Topics: Pastoral leadership, Church governance, Ministry administration, Team leadership, Leadership development

MTH 502 – Spiritual Formation & Discipleship 4 Credits

Examines the principles of spiritual formation and intentional discipleship. Students learn to facilitate spiritual growth in others, develop systems for discipleship multiplication, and cultivate spiritual disciplines for personal and corporate transformation.

Topics: Spiritual formation, Discipleship, Spiritual disciplines, Multiplication, Spiritual growth

MTH 503 – Strategic Ministry Leadership & Planning 4 Credits

Develops skills in strategic ministry leadership, vision casting, and organizational development. Students learn to lead ministry organizations with vision, strategy, and Kingdom impact through effective planning and implementation.

Topics: Strategic leadership, Vision casting, Organizational development, Ministry planning, Implementation strategies

MTH 504 – Homiletics & Advanced Preaching 4 Credits

Advanced training in biblical preaching and teaching. Covers homiletical methods, sermon preparation, expository preaching, narrative preaching, and effective communication strategies for diverse ministry contexts.

Topics: Homiletics, Expository preaching, Sermon preparation, Narrative preaching, Communication strategies

MTH 505 – Pastoral Counseling & Soul Care 4 Credits

Equips students with pastoral counseling skills for effective soul care. Covers biblical counseling models, crisis intervention, grief counseling, marriage and family counseling, and ethical considerations in pastoral care.

Topics: Pastoral counseling, Soul care, Crisis intervention, Grief counseling, Marriage and family counseling

MTH 506 – Church Growth & Multiplication 4 Credits

Explores principles of church growth, church planting, and multiplication movements. Students learn strategies for developing healthy growing churches, planting new congregations, and fostering multiplication within ministry contexts.

Topics: Church growth, Church planting, Multiplication, Healthy church development, Missional strategies

MTH 507 – Theology & Ministry Practice 4 Credits

Integrates systematic theology with practical ministry application. Students explore key theological doctrines and their implications for contemporary ministry, including theological reflection, contextualization, and applied theology.

Topics: Systematic theology, Applied theology, Theological reflection, Contextualization, Doctrine and practice

MTH 508 – Capstone: Ministry Leadership Project 3 Credits

Integrative capstone project applying all program learning. Students develop a comprehensive ministry plan or project that integrates pastoral leadership, spiritual formation, discipleship, strategic planning, and theological reflection for a real-world ministry challenge.

Topics: Capstone project, Ministry plan development, Strategic integration, Real-world application, Leadership synthesis

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How long does the Advanced Professional Diploma in Ministry take?

The program takes 6-9 months to complete with 8 core courses.

❓ Is the program fully online?

Yes, the program is 100% online and can be completed from anywhere in the world.

❓ Are scholarships available?

Yes, we offer a 100% full tuition-waiver scholarship for eligible candidates through foundational grants.

❓ Who should take this program?

This program is ideal for pastors, ministry leaders, church planters, missionaries, and those seeking advanced leadership in ministry.

❓ What career opportunities does this diploma provide?

Graduates can serve as Senior Pastors, Executive Pastors, Ministry Directors, Church Planters, Missions Directors, and Theological Educators.
