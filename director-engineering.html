Mr. Tabaka Mochipela

Director, School of Engineering & Technology
Living Oracles Global Institute (LOGI)

Mr. Tabaka Mochipela serves as the Director of the School of Engineering & Technology at Living Oracles Global Institute (LOGI). He is a cybersecurity and digital forensics professional with expertise spanning network security, computer forensics, information systems management, and cybersecurity operations.

With an academic and professional background in information systems and cybersecurity, Mr. Mochipela brings a practical, technology-focused perspective to the development of LOGI's programmes. His work is centred on equipping students and professionals with relevant knowledge, technical competencies, and problem-solving skills needed to operate effectively in an increasingly digital world.

"We believe our institution can become different. We are not building 'Online Institute → Courses → Videos → Quiz → Certificate.' We are building: Learn → Practise → Build → Solve → Portfolio → Certificate." — Mr. Tabaka Mochipela

Academic & Professional Background

Mr. Mochipela holds a Master of Science (MSc) in Information Systems Management and a Bachelor of Science (BSc Hons) in Network Security & Computer Forensics. He is also a Certified Forensic Analyst with professional expertise in cybersecurity and digital forensics.

His areas of professional interest include cybersecurity strategy, digital investigations, network protection, information systems, and the application of technology to address contemporary organisational and security challenges.

Leadership at LOGI

As Director of the School of Engineering & Technology, Mr. Mochipela contributes to the academic and strategic development of LOGI's technology-focused programmes. His responsibilities include:

  • Academic and curriculum development
  • Technology programme coordination
  • Faculty collaboration and development
  • Industry-oriented learning initiatives
  • Student mentorship and academic guidance
  • Development of practical and skills-based technology education

Through his leadership, the School of Engineering & Technology seeks to provide students with an educational experience that combines academic knowledge, practical application, professional development, and ethical responsibility.
