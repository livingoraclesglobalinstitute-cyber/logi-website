Internship Agreement

Terms of the Internship Placement

This is a student learning and professional-development placement at Living Oracles Global Institute (LOGI), not a regular employment position. By accepting, you agree to the following:

  1. To participate in the internship on a full-time remote basis for the initial duration of three (3) months, subject to progress reviews.
  2. To complete all assigned supervised tasks, learning activities and practical assignments to the best of your ability.
  3. To accept guidance and feedback from your assigned LOGI supervisor and participate in progress reviews during and at the end of the placement.
  4. To maintain confidentiality of all institutional records, student data and internal information accessed during the placement.
  5. To provide your own reliable internet access and a suitable working environment.
  6. To communicate promptly and professionally with LOGI staff and fellow interns.
  7. To understand that any remuneration or allowance arrangements, if applicable, will be explained clearly before the placement is confirmed.
  8. To understand that completion documentation may be issued only after meeting the programme requirements.
  9. To notify LOGI immediately if you are unable to continue the placement.
